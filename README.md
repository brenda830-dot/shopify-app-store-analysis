# shopify-app-store-analysis
1. Executive Summary & Purpose
This document serves as both a high-level briefing for business decision-makers and technical onboarding documentation for incoming analysts.

The primary objective of this Power BI report is to consolidate operational and financial data into a single source of truth. It allows stakeholders to track key performance indicators (KPIs), identify performance bottlenecks, and monitor regional/product-line trends in real time.

2. Business Objectives & Key Metrics
The report tracks several core business KPIs across three standard reporting layers:

Executive Pulse (Page 1): High-level operational summary tracking Gross Margin, Revenue Growth (YoY), and Customer Acquisition/Churn Rates.

Segment & Regional Diagnostics (Page 2): Breakdown of volume and profitability by product category, sales channel, and territory.

Detail / Drill-Through (Page 3): Transaction-level audit views supporting variance investigation and operational reconciliations.
